    Construction sequence

    How we build

    The same six stages on every Vedha site. At the end of each stage you get photos and the engineer's checklist before the next payment is due.

    1

    Site visit & soil test

    Survey, levels and soil bearing capacity check before any design.

    Week 0 · Free
    2

    Drawings, 3D & BOQ

    Plan, elevation, 3D view, structural drawings and an itemised bill of quantities.

    Week 1–2
    3

    Approvals

    Help with local body plan approval and utility connections.

    As required
    4

    Foundation

    Excavation, PCC, footings and plinth beam with anti-termite treatment.

    Cube test 7 / 28 day
    5

    Superstructure

    Columns, beams, slabs and brickwork, with slab curing for at least 14 days.

    Slump checked per pour
    6

    Finishes & handover

    Plastering, flooring, plumbing, electrical and painting, then a joint snag walk.

    12-month defect cover

    Questions families ask

    Before you build

    Don't see your question? Call or WhatsApp Manivasagan on +91 73390 00678.

    How much does it cost to build a house in Chidambaram?

    It depends on the type of work and finishes. Use the estimator above for a first figure. After the free site visit we give you an itemised BOQ listing every quantity, rate and brand, so you can compare it line by line.

    Can I see my home in 3D before construction?

    Yes. After the site visit and plan, we prepare elevation drawings and a 3D view of your home so you can see it from every side before work starts.

    How long does a duplex house take?

    A typical G+1 duplex of 1,800–3,000 sq.ft takes about 8 to 12 months from foundation to handover, depending on design, finishes and approvals.

    Do you help with plan approval?

    Yes. We prepare the floor plans, elevations and structural drawings and help you with local body plan approval and utility connections.

    How does payment work?

    Payment is stage-wise. Each stage is finished and checked by our engineer, you receive photos and a checklist, and only then the next payment falls due.

    Can I choose my own brands and materials?

    Yes. The BOQ lists brands for cement, steel, tiles, sanitaryware and electricals. You can choose alternatives and we update the quote before work starts.

    Do you take projects outside Chidambaram?

    Our office is in Annamalai Nagar, Chidambaram. For sites in nearby towns, call us with the location and we will confirm.
